# Atlas Creator Bridge — local release candidate

Creator Bridge is a manual content-and-outcome ledger for TSC. It helps one piece of work travel through a simple loop: plan the story → post it yourself → invite one clear next step → follow up → record what came from it.

## Use it

1. Open **Creator Bridge** from the Atlas navigation and unlock the local workspace.
2. Choose one real piece of work already in progress. Set its audience and purpose (show work, find collaborators, sell a creative commission, find paid service work, or build community).
3. Choose the TSC pillars and the channels you intend to use. Add the caption, a source asset link, and a single call to action. Link the content to its TSC project when useful.
4. Save. You publish the post manually and paste its live links back into Atlas. The system does not connect to social accounts or post on your behalf.
5. After publishing, enter counts you can verify from the platform and log real inquiries, paid project count, and collected revenue. The summary only totals values you enter; it does not claim to verify them.
6. Use **Add follow-up task** to keep the response work visible on Today. Use Workspace to keep a contact record and connect the relationship to an existing project.

## TSC first experiment

Start in Albuquerque and the Southwest. Turn one authentic TSC work-in-progress into a small series: the process, the finished detail, who contributed, and one invitation. A workable initial invitation is a small, scoped artist collaboration or a paid visual commission; state which one it is, what the other person receives, what you need from them, the deadline, and how both sides may share the result. Seek consent before featuring someone or their work. Do not present an idea as a confirmed collaboration.

Keep the wider TSC identity intact: art, music, fashion, auto, technology and education connect under one street-lux culture house. The public invitation can be to follow, collaborate, commission, or join the email list. Do not sell a future Academy, event, sponsor, or global operation as if it already exists.

## What it does not do

- It does not search for artists, predict who will collaborate, send DMs or emails, schedule or publish content, process payments, or pull platform analytics.
- It does not manufacture leads, guarantee audience growth or income, or prove demand outside the people and transactions you record.
- The local SQLite database is unencrypted. Store work metadata only; do not add sensitive credentials or payment data.

## Proof standard

Separate reach from business outcomes. A view is not a fan, an inquiry is not a sale, and a planned collaboration is not a confirmed one. The useful weekly questions are: Which story created a real conversation? Which invitation got a reply? Which inquiry became paid work? What did delivery cost in time and materials? What should be repeated, changed or stopped?

The Creator Bridge is an unmerged, undeployed feature-branch build until its pull request is reviewed and merged. Browser/device rendering, external publishing and platform analytics are not verified by the backend test suite.
