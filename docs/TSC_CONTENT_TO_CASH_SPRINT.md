# TSC Content-to-Cash Sprint

## Goal

Create a repeatable Albuquerque-first path from authentic TSC work to a real conversation and a paid pilot. This is a test plan, not a revenue forecast.

## Keep the offer small

Test one clearly scoped **artist-release visual package**: original single/EP cover artwork plus a small set of matching social-story assets. Confirm the artist, deadline, deliverable dimensions, number of revisions, credit, usage permissions, payment and delivery in writing before work starts. Price from the actual hours, costs and capacity; do not price by copying an unsupported online rate. If a person wants to collaborate rather than commission, define the contribution, who owns and may use each asset, and where both sides will share it before starting.

Do not announce this as an established TSC service until the first paid pilot is delivered. Start with one artist and one finished example. Avoid making merch or funding an event before paid demand exists.

## Make one piece travel

Choose existing original work rather than opening several new projects. Use one source asset (art, an approved music section, a design render, or a real process clip) to make:

1. **Process** — how the work is being made.
2. **Detail** — one design or sound choice and why it matters.
3. **Reveal** — the finished piece, with the collaborator credited only with permission.
4. **Invitation** — one specific next step, such as a paid visual commission or one scoped collaboration.

Keep the TSC voice and visual identity consistent across art, music, fashion, auto and Atlas. Select one primary audience and one CTA for each post rather than asking the audience to follow, buy, collaborate and sponsor all at once.

## Outreach that respects the artist

Pick a small number of Albuquerque/Southwest artists whose work actually connects to the post. Read and respond to their work specifically. Send a personal invitation only after the example is ready. Example structure:

> Your [specific work/detail] caught my eye because [real reason]. I’m building TSC in Albuquerque, connecting art and music through finished work. I’m testing one [paid visual commission / clearly scoped collaboration]: [deliverable and deadline]. I can show you [link to proof]. If that fits what you’re making, would you be open to a short conversation? No pressure if it isn’t a fit.

Do not mass-DM, imply a partnership before it is agreed, or promise reach. If the first message is unanswered, make one respectful follow-up, then move on.

## Four-week test

- **Week 1:** Pick the best existing sample; make one offer page or simple portfolio post; write down actual time and cost to deliver it.
- **Weeks 2–4:** Publish one process/reveal story each week. Use YouTube Shorts and the channels where the intended collaborators already share work. Personally invite a small, hand-selected set of artists to discuss the specific pilot. Log each post and next action in Creator Bridge; contacts and tasks stay in Workspace/Today.
- **Every week:** Check replies and follow up once; record inquiries, conversations, quotes, paid projects, collected revenue, delivery time and costs. Keep consent for images, music and collaborator credits.

## Decision at the end

Repeat only if there is evidence: at least one serious inquiry or paid pilot, the deliverable can be completed without disrupting core work, and actual costs/time leave a viable margin. If there is attention but no inquiry, change the offer or CTA. If there is no relevant reach, change where or how the story is shared. Do not call views, compliments, informal promises or planned work revenue.

## Revenue reality

Treat direct paid creative work and existing paid automotive/technical work as near-term cash paths; treat social growth as distribution and trust, not immediate payroll. Platform revenue is a later milestone. As of 2026-10-22, YouTube's expanded YPP allows eligible creators in supported countries to apply for some earlier fan-funding/Shopping features at 500 subscribers, 3 recent public uploads, and either 3,000 qualified watch hours or 3 million Shorts views; full ad revenue has the higher 1,000-subscriber plus 4,000-watch-hour or 10-million-Shorts-view threshold, and still requires review. Always verify current eligibility in YouTube Studio before planning around it.

## Tracker boundary

Atlas Creator Bridge is only a manual ledger. It does not discover artists, contact them, publish, collect analytics, or process money. A person approves and performs every external action. See [Creator Bridge](CREATOR_BRIDGE.md) for the workflow and limitations.
